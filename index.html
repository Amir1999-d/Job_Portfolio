Holiday Transit Equity in Milan — An Open-Data Framework

A transferable, fully open-data framework quantifying the structural temporal blind spots in transit-equity standards. 1. The 5-Phase Methodology[cite: 2]
  • Vulnerability Index: Composite ratio derived from $V_i = x \cdot \text{elderly}_i + y \cdot \text{foreign}_i + (1-x-y) \cdot \text{single-household}_i$ anchoring demographic needs[cite: 2].
  • Supply Burden: Quantifies headway metrics from trips-per-day over explicit day-types[cite: 2].
  • Disaggregation: Resolves individual holiday profiles to parse structural reduction profiles[cite: 2].
  • Essential Access: Validates polygon reachability boundaries (Pharmacy AND Supermarket AND Hospital)[cite: 2].
  • Ceiling Optimization: Executes budget-neutral reallocation under operational floors[cite: 2].
2. Case Study Discoveries[cite: 2]
  • Temporal Shifts: Median headways stretch from 10.1 min (weekdays) to 20.0 min (holidays), inducing a stark evening cliff collapse after 20:00 where 330 peripheral stops lose service[cite: 2].
  • The Christmas Anomaly: Civil holidays remain strictly uniform ($\pm$0.7 min variance), but Christmas service drops by a critical 45%[cite: 2].
  • Double Exclusion: 12.1% of municipal stops exhibit severe top-quartile service drops compounded by walk-up essential network failures[cite: 2].
3. Optimization & Policy[cite: 2]
  • Reallocation Mechanics: Adjusting 275 urban donor stops unlocks a substantial 17.8 min wait-time dividend for 320 recipient stops[cite: 2].
  • Saturation Floor: Reallocation efficiency caps out at a 44% ceiling before recipients hit operational limits[cite: 2].
  • Direct Policy Actions: 1. Enforce a 30-min headways KPI threshold for high-need zones; 2. Deploy the validated 20% redistribution scenario across 155 high-priority stops; 3. Transmit standalone Christmas schedule notices 2 weeks in advance[cite: 2].
