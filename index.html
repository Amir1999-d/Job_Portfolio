MSc Thesis 2025–2026 — In Progress
Holiday Transit Equity in Milan — An Open-Data Framework

A transferable, fully open-data pipeline quantifying how social vulnerability maps against public holiday service cuts across ATM Milan's network. Combines GTFS schedule data, ISTAT 2021 census demographics, and OpenStreetMap essential-service geometry to build a Holiday Need Index — then applies a ceiling-bounded Robin Hood reallocation model to reduce distributional inequity at zero additional cost to the operator.

Methodology
  • Vulnerability Index: Composite of elderly share, foreign resident share, and single-household density from ISTAT 2021 — anchors demographic need at stop level.
  • Supply Burden: Headway metrics computed from GTFS trips-per-day, disaggregated by day type — weekday, Sunday, and individual public holidays.
  • Essential Access: 500m walk-up buffers to pharmacies, supermarkets, and hospitals from OpenStreetMap, identifying stops with compounded service and access failure.
  • Ceiling Optimiser: Budget-neutral Robin Hood reallocation under operational headway floors — efficiency caps at 44% donor reduction.
Key Findings
  • Temporal pattern: Median headway stretches from 10.1 min (weekdays) to 20.0 min (holidays); 330 peripheral stops lose service entirely after 20:00.
  • Christmas anomaly: Civil holidays show ±0.7 min headway variance, but Christmas service drops 45% — structurally different from all other holidays.
  • Double exclusion: 319 stops (12.1% of network) combine top-quartile service collapse with no walkable essential services — concentrated in Quarto Oggiaro, Corvetto, Forlanini.
  • Midday penalty: Widest equity gap occurs 10:00–16:00, not at rush hour — when vulnerable groups most need social and essential-service access.
Policy Outputs
  • Reallocation: 275 donor stops → 320 recipient stops; each redistributed trip delivers 6× more wait-time reduction to high-need stops than it costs donors.
  • Optimal threshold: 20% donor reduction maximises Gini improvement (22.9%) before diminishing returns set in at the 44% ceiling.
  • Three recommendations: Enforce 30-min headway KPI floor for high-need zones; deploy 20% redistribution scenario across 155 priority stops; issue advance Christmas schedule notices 2 weeks prior.
  • Replicability: Framework is fully open-data — transferable to any Italian city with GTFS + ISTAT access.
